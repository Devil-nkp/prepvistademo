🔧 Technical & Platform
Does PrepVista support Tamil? What languages are available?
+
Yes. The platform interface and support communications are available in both English and Tamil. Can students use this on low-end smartphones? What internet speed is needed?
+
Yes. PrepVista runs on any Android or iOS smartphone browser — no app installation required. It has been optimised for Indian mobile networks: a standard 4G connection is sufficient. Students in hostels or areas with moderate connectivity can use the platform without performance issues. If a student can open YouTube on their phone, they can use PrepVista.
Can we bulk upload students? Can we export reports for NAAC?
+
Yes to both. The admin panel accepts a CSV with student email, name, ID, department, year, batch, and section. Upload 500 students in under 5 minutes. For reports, the analytics page lets you filter by any segment and export a CSV showing each student's sessions, average score, and best score — formatted for NAAC Criterion 5 documentation.
🏫 Practical & Implementation
Will students actually use this? We have tried platforms before that nobody opened.
+
This is the most important question. Three things drive consistent usage: fear, convenience, and visible results. Fear of placement failure is already present — we don't need to manufacture motivation. Convenience is guaranteed — the platform works on any phone at any hour. Visible results: the moment a student sees their personalised score after their first session, they return. Unlike passive video content, an AI interview that asks about your actual resume project creates immediate engagement. Your TPO also has a live usage dashboard — if a student isn't practising, you know and can intervene. The platform also supports making sessions mandatory as part of placement registration.
We already have a training department. Why do we need this?
+
PrepVista does not replace your training faculty — it multiplies their impact 100×. Your TPO is the cricket coach. PrepVista is the nets facility where players practice daily, individually, with data on every ball they face. The coach handles strategy, relationships, and human mentoring. PrepVista handles the 500-student-scale individual practice problem that no human team can solve alone. Human expertise + AI scale is dramatically more effective than either alone.
Who owns the student data? Is it secure?
+
Your institution's student data belongs to your institution. PrepVista does not share student data with any third party — including recruiters — without explicit permission from the college admin. All data is encrypted at rest and in transit. Student interview recordings and performance reports are accessible only to the student and the college admin accounts you designate. A complete data handling policy document is available on request before any commitment.
